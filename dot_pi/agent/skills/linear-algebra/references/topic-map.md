# Matrix Computations topic map

Use this file for fast orientation before opening the full book.

- Chapter 1 — Matrix multiplication
  - basic matrix/vector operations, BLAS levels, block algorithms, locality, parallel multiplication
- Chapter 2 — Matrix analysis
  - norms, SVD, subspace metrics, conditioning, finite precision
- Chapter 3 — General linear systems
  - triangular solves, LU, roundoff, pivoting, iterative refinement, parallel LU
- Chapter 4 — Special linear systems
  - diagonal dominance, symmetric positive definite systems, banded systems, symmetric indefinite systems, Toeplitz, circulant, Poisson
- Chapter 5 — Orthogonalization and least squares
  - Householder, Givens, QR, full-rank and rank-deficient least squares
- Chapter 6 — Modified least squares problems and methods
  - weighting, regularization, constrained least squares, total least squares, updating factorizations
- Chapter 7 — Unsymmetric eigenvalue problems
  - perturbation theory, power iteration, Hessenberg form, Schur form, practical QR, invariant subspaces, generalized eigenproblems, pseudospectra
- Chapter 8 — Symmetric eigenvalue problems
  - power iterations, symmetric QR, tridiagonal methods, Jacobi methods, SVD computation, generalized symmetric eigenproblems
- Chapter 9 — Functions of matrices
  - matrix exponential, sign function, square root, logarithm
- Chapter 10 — Large sparse eigenvalue problems
  - Lanczos, quadrature, sparse SVD, unsymmetric Krylov methods, Jacobi-Davidson
- Chapter 11 — Large sparse linear system problems
  - direct sparse methods, stationary iterations, conjugate gradient, Krylov methods, preconditioning, multigrid
- Chapter 12 — Special topics
  - displacement structure, structured-rank problems, Kronecker products, tensor unfoldings and contractions
